A. Certificate Requests

Apply for official Gram Panchayat documents and civil status certificates without visiting offices.

Residence Certificate

Official proof of residence in the village / gram panchayat jurisdiction for legal and academic purposes.

⏱️ 3-5 Working Days Apply Now

Income Certificate

Income verification certificate for government welfare schemes, student scholarships, and subsidies.

⏱️ 3-5 Working Days Apply Now

Birth Certificate

Official record and certification of birth registration issued by the local panchayat authority.

⏱️ 2-4 Working Days Apply Now

Death Certificate

Certified death registration record issued for inheritance, administrative, and legal settlement purposes.

⏱️ 2-4 Working Days Apply Now

Caste Certificate

Application for local panchayat endorsement and recommendation for official caste certification.

⏱️ 5-7 Working Days Apply Now

Other Certificate

No-objection certificates (NOC), character verification, and other customized local certificates.

⏱️ 3-5 Working Days Apply Now

Water Supply

⚡ High Priority Report Issue

Streetlight Repair

⚡ 24-48 Hours Report Issue

Road Repair

Garbage Collection

⚡ 24-48 Hours Report Issue

Drainage Problem

Report clogged drains, waterlogging in public streets, or sewer maintenance issues.
